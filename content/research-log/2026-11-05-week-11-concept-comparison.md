## What I did
- Compared the three concepts on four criteria: engineering depth, Spring prototype feasibility, ease of performance validation, and full-sneaker integration
- Gave each concept a preliminary High / Medium / Low rating per criterion (my judgment, not measured scores)
- Chose a current first choice to bring to Dr. Allen

## What I found
- [Assumption] Lattice midsole: depth High, feasibility Medium, validation High, integration Medium
- [Assumption] Achilles-focused shoe: depth High, feasibility Medium, validation Low, integration Medium
- [Assumption] Modular arch support: depth Medium, feasibility High, validation Medium–High, integration High

## Engineering decisions
- Picked the 3D-printed lattice midsole as my current first choice because:
  - Lattices make it easiest to experiment with structure and design
  - Current lattice design tools make changing the structure easier
  - It's simpler from a production standpoint
- Kept the other two concepts as alternatives. The final direction depends on advisor feedback, access to fabrication equipment, and testing capability

## Problems and failures
- Lattice risks to work through: printing flexible materials, long print times, durability, and attaching the midsole to the upper
