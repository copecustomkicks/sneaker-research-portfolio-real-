## What I did

- Met with Dr. Allen to go over my plan and outline for fall research.
- Talked through how the teardown work could connect to force measurement.
- Discussed the advisory committee for my honors thesis and the advisory committee for my realization thesis class.

## Engineering decisions

- Keep the teardown plan, but run force testing on each sneaker *before* cutting it open, so the shoes are intact when measured.
- Use two measurement sources: force-sensing insoles and force plates.
- Use the difference between the two force measurements to calculate ground reaction force.
- Compare ground reaction force across a few different sneakers and look for correlations with the material used in the sole.

## Mentor feedback

- Dr. Allen liked the plan/outline as written, including the teardown approach.
- She suggested extending it to compare the force felt across different sneakers rather than stopping at teardown.
- Direction for the fall materials research will follow from this force comparison work.
