## Objectives for the week

- Practice component-callout and construction-detail-callout labeling on a second real shoe, extending the conventions used for the Nike Kobe 9 and Air Jordan 1 Low Voodoo studies.
- Note any component terms that come up for the first time.

## Activities completed

- Completed a two-part callout exercise on a Puma RS-X:
  - **Component callouts (20 items):** toe tip, lateral vamp overlay, vamp, lower eyestay, laces, lace keeper, upper eyestay, tongue tab, eyelet, tongue, collar lining, heel tab/collar, heel loop, heel clip, heel overlay, lateral logo, quarter, quarter overlay, midsole, outsole.
  - **Construction-detail callouts (6 items):** double stitching, hole punchout eyelets, single stitching, molded outsole, perforated holes in vamp, stitched logo.
- Unlike the Kobe 9 and AJ1 Voodoo studies, the base image for this exercise was a reference photograph found online, not an illustration I traced or rendered myself.

## Engineering decisions

- Only embed a shoe image on this site when I created or traced the base illustration myself, or photographed my own physical item. A found or stock photograph of a current commercial product gets cited and described in text instead, the same way the Motawi textbook pages were cited without reproducing the scanned page images. That's why this entry lists the callout labels in text rather than showing the photo.

## Evidence and artifacts

- The 20 component-callout and 6 construction-detail-callout labels above, applied to a Puma RS-X reference photograph (not reproduced on this site — see Engineering decisions).

## Reflection

This exercise added a noticeably different silhouette to my labeling practice — the RS-X's chunky retro-runner proportions and layered overlay panels read very differently from the Kobe 9 or the AJ1 Low. A few terms came up for the first time: heel clip, lace keeper, and tongue tab aren't in my current anatomy reference. Worth folding into that reference later if they turn out to be standard vocabulary rather than Puma-specific naming.

## Next steps

- Continue building component vocabulary across different shoe styles and construction types.
- Evaluate whether heel clip, lace keeper, and tongue tab should be added to the site's anatomy reference once I've seen them used consistently elsewhere.
